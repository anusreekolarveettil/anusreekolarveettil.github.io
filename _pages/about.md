I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, working with Prof. G. Madhavi Latha. My research looks at how particle shape controls the behaviour of granular soils, combining 3D printing, X-ray micro-computed tomography, laboratory experiments and discrete element modelling (DEM). I submitted my thesis in November 2026 and am currently looking for postdoctoral opportunities.

Research
======
My PhD thesis, *Multi-level Particle Morphology Effects on Granular Soil Behaviour Using 3D Printed Granular Media and Numerical Simulations*, establishes 3D printing as a way to separate the effects of particle shape from other soil properties. The work covers:

- **Printing natural sand shapes:** how faithfully SLA, DLP and PolyJet printing reproduce the morphology of natural sand particles, measured with micro-CT scanning and image analysis
- **Packing and permeability:** how particle form and angularity affect the packing and permeability of 3D-printed sand surrogates 
- **Transparent soil:** transparent sand made from 3D-printed particles, with pore-scale flow measured by particle image velocimetry (PIV) in a custom setup
- **DEM simulations:** discrete element modelling in PFC3D of how macro- and meso-scale particle shape affects dense and loose packings, validated against experiments

Before my PhD, I completed an MTech at IIT Madras, where my thesis studied the seismic response of mechanically stabilised earth (MSE) walls.
